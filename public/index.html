What can I type?

A card name, or part of one. Matching cards come back most-played first.

Scryfall syntax. Colour (c:, id:), type (t:), rules text (o:, kw:), mana value, power, toughness and loyalty (mv, pow, tou, loy), mana cost (m:2WW), devotion and the mana a card makes (produces:g), legality (f:, banned:), tags (otag:), is: flags, - to negate, or, parentheses and regular expressions.

Printings. Set (set:), rarity (r:), artist (a:), flavour text (ft:), frame, border and game, year and date, price (usd<1), and whether a card was ever printed somewhere (in:). A card is shown by a printing that matches. Add unique:prints to list every printing, or unique:art for every illustration.

Plain English. Describe the card. An opening such as "two mana blue instant" is applied as a filter.

Not yet: languages other than English, art tags, and a few shortcuts such as is:fetchland.
