      What can I type?

      A card name, or part of one. Matching cards come back most-played first.

      Scryfall syntax. Colour (c:, id:), type (t:), rules text (o:, kw:), mana value, power, toughness and loyalty (mv, pow, tou, loy), mana cost (m:2WW), devotion and the mana a card makes (produces:g), legality (f:, banned:), tags (otag:), is: flags and shortcuts (is:fetchland, is:commander, is:modal), - to negate, or, parentheses and regular expressions (o:/gets \spp/ finds "gets +2/+2").

      Printings. Set (set:), rarity (r:), artist (a:), flavour text (ft:), frame, border and game, year and date, price (usd<1), and whether a card was ever printed somewhere (in:). A card is shown by a printing that matches. Add unique:prints to list every printing, or unique:art for every illustration.

      Plain English. Describe the card. An opening such as "two mana blue instant" is applied as a filter.

      Not yet: languages other than English, art tags, blocks and cubes. A term that cannot be used is left out, the page says which, and the rest of the search runs.
