Plain English

Describe the card the way you remember it. Words about cost, colour and type at the start are applied as a filter, and the rest is matched by meaning, so the wording does not have to be the card's.

sphinx that draws two cards
A description of what the card does.
two mana blue instant that counters any spell
Cost, colour and type are read from the opening words.
dwarf destroys lands
A few words are enough.

Names

Words on their own look in the card's name, ignoring case, accents and punctuation. A card's whole name opens that card's page; part of a name lists the cards that have it, the most played first.

lightning bolt
A card's whole name: its page opens.
bolt
Part of a name: every card with it in the name.
!"lightning bolt" unique:prints
An exclamation mark asks for that exact name, here for all of its printings.
name:/^the /
A regular expression on the name.

Colours and colour identity

c: is a card's colours and id: its colour identity. Use letters (w u b r g), words, or the name of a guild, shard or wedge. A bare c: means at least these colours; a bare id: means within them, as for a Commander deck.

c:uw t:instant
Instants that are at least white and blue.
c=rg t:dragon
Dragons that are exactly red and green.
id:esper is:commander
Commanders that fit in a white, blue and black deck.
c:m t:angel
Multicoloured Angels. c:c is colourless, c=2 is exactly two colours.

Types

t: looks in the type line: card types, supertypes and subtypes.

t:sphinx
Every Sphinx.
t:"legendary creature" t:elf
A phrase in quotes, and more than one type.
kw:flash -t:creature -t:land
A minus sign in front of a term means not.

Rules text and keywords

o: looks in the rules text without reminder text; fo: includes it. ~ stands for the card's own name. kw: is a keyword ability.

o:"draw two cards" c:u
A phrase in the rules text.
o:"~ deals" t:instant mv=1
A card that names itself.
kw:deathtouch c:g mv<=2
A keyword ability.

Mana cost and mana value

mv is the mana value, with = < > <= >= !=. m: is the cost itself: a bare colon means at least these symbols. devotion: and produces: ask what a card adds to devotion and what mana it makes.

mv<=2 t:creature pow>=3
Cheap creatures that hit hard.
m:2WW
A cost with at least two generic and two white.
m={R/W}{R/W}
Exactly this cost, hybrid symbols included.
devotion:bb t:zombie
Zombies that add two to devotion to black.
produces:g t:creature mv=1
One-mana creatures that make green mana.

Power, toughness and loyalty

pow, tou and loy compare with a number, and power and toughness with each other.

pow>=7 mv<=4
Big for the cost.
pow>tou t:goblin mv=1
More power than toughness.
loy>=6 t:planeswalker
Planeswalkers that start high.

Sets, rarity and printings

These are about a printing of a card: its set, rarity, artist, flavour text, frame, border and date. A card is shown by a printing that matches. in: asks whether a card was ever printed in a set, at a rarity or in a game.

set:mh2 r:mythic
Mythic rares of Modern Horizons 2.
a:"mark zug"
Illustrated by an artist.
ft:dragon t:goblin
Words in the flavour text.
year=2015 t:planeswalker r>=rare
Released in a year; rarity has an order.
in:paper -in:mtgo t:elf r:rare
Printed on paper but never on Magic Online.
is:foil frame:1997 t:goblin
Printing flags, such as foil, promo, reprint and borderless.

Prices

usd, eur and tix compare a printing's price. Prices come from Scryfall's daily data.

t:dragon r:mythic usd<5
Mythic Dragons under five dollars.
usd>=50 t:land
Lands at fifty dollars or more.
t:sphinx order:usd direction:desc
The dearest first.

Formats

f: is legality in a format; banned: and restricted: are the lists.

f:pauper t:elf mv=1
Legal in Pauper.
banned:modern t:artifact
Banned in Modern.
restricted:vintage
Restricted in Vintage.

Shortcuts and tags

is: covers well-known groups: land cycles, kinds of commander, vanilla creatures, modal spells and more. otag: uses Scryfall's community tags for what a card does.

is:fetchland
A land cycle by its nickname. Also shockland, checkland, triome and twenty more.
is:commander t:angel mv<=4
Cards that can be a commander.
otag:removal c:w mv=1 t:instant
A community tag.
is:modal t:instant c:b mv=2
Spells that offer a choice of modes.

Regular expressions

Put a pattern between slashes after o:, fo:, t:, name: or ft:. Scryfall's shorthands work: \spp is +X/+X, \smm is -X/-X, \spt is X/X, \sm is any mana symbol.

o:/draw (two|three) cards/ t:sorcery mv<=3
Either wording.
t:instant o:/\spp/ mv=1 c:g
Green one-mana instants that give +X/+X.
o:/^\ss: draw a card\.$/ t:artifact
An ability that is one symbol and nothing else.

Combining terms

Terms side by side must all hold. or means either, brackets group, and a minus sign in front of a term or a group means not.

t:elf or t:goblin
Either type.
(t:elf or t:goblin) mv=1 -c:r
A group, another condition, and not red.
c:u t:sphinx draws when opponents draw
Keywords and plain words together: the words rank what the keywords find.

What a result is, and its order

A result is a card unless you say otherwise. These can also be chosen with the controls above the results; what is typed in the query wins.

!"lightning bolt" unique:prints order:released
Every printing of a card, newest first.
!"serra angel" unique:art
Every illustration.
t:sphinx order:cmc direction:desc
Sorted by mana value, highest first.
t:sphinx prefer:oldest
Each card shown by its oldest printing.

Not supported yet: other languages (lang:), new:, block:, cube: and art tags (art:). A term that cannot be used is left out, the page says which term and why, and the rest of the search runs.
